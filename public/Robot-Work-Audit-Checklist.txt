# AIGENCY AUTOMATA: 47-Point Robot Work Audit Checklist
## Find the "Robot Work" hiding in your workflows.

This checklist is designed to help business owners, sales leaders, and ops managers identify repetitive, low-leverage tasks that should be handled by custom AI agents. 

### Instructions:
Check every box that applies to your current team. If you check more than 10, your team is likely wasting 15+ hours per week on "Robot Work."

---

### [ ] SECTION 1: LEAD GENERATION & MARKETING
1. [ ] Manual lead entry from website forms into CRM.
2. [ ] Sending "Thanks for your interest" emails manually.
3. [ ] Qualifying leads by manually checking LinkedIn profiles or websites.
4. [ ] Categorizing inbound leads by industry, size, or budget.
5. [ ] Manually adding leads to email marketing sequences (Mailchimp, HubSpot, etc.).
6. [ ] Checking for and merging duplicate leads across different platforms.
7. [ ] Hand-typing notifications to sales reps about new high-intent leads.
8. [ ] Posting the same content across multiple social channels manually.
9. [ ] Responding to basic FAQ comments on social media/ads.
10. [ ] Tracking ad spend vs. lead volume in a manual daily/weekly spreadsheet.

### [ ] SECTION 2: SALES & CRM HYGIENE
11. [ ] Updating lead status/tags in CRM after every call.
12. [ ] Manually scheduling follow-up tasks for yourself or reps.
13. [ ] Creating and sending personalized meeting invites and calendar links.
14. [ ] Transcribing call notes and recording them into CRM fields.
15. [ ] Searching for prospect email addresses or phone numbers one-by-one.
16. [ ] Manually drafting "personalized" outreach based on recent LinkedIn activity.
17. [ ] Moving deals between pipeline stages manually in your CRM.
18. [ ] Generating sales contracts or proposals from templates by copy-pasting details.
19. [ ] Following up with "ghosted" leads who didn't show up for a demo.
20. [ ] Periodic manual cleanup of dead or inactive leads in the database.

### [ ] SECTION 3: OPERATIONS & WORKFLOW
21. [ ] Rescuing data from one tool to place it in another (The "Copy-Paste Cycle").
22. [ ] Manually assigning project tasks to team members based on new orders.
23. [ ] Chasing team members for project status updates.
24. [ ] Filing digital documents, receipts, or contracts into specific folders.
25. [ ] Managing permissions/access for new team members across various apps.
26. [ ] Updating client project boards (Trello, Asana, Monday) manually.
27. [ ] Cross-checking data between two systems to identify and fix errors.
28. [ ] Sending internal reminders for upcoming or missed deadlines.
29. [ ] Manually formatting data/reports for internal weekly syncs.
30. [ ] Monitoring generic inboxes (info@, support@) just to route emails.

### [ ] SECTION 4: CUSTOMER SUCCESS & ONBOARDING
31. [ ] Sending onboarding questionnaires to new clients manually.
32. [ ] Creating shared folders, Slack channels, or portals for new clients.
33. [ ] Manually setting up new client accounts in your internal software.
34. [ ] Sending physical or digital welcome gifts/packages.
35. [ ] Manually verifying if a client has completed their first "Action Step."
36. [ ] Sending manual "How's it going?" check-in emails.
37. [ ] Requesting reviews or testimonials manually after a project closes.
38. [ ] Handling Support tickets that have standard, repetitive answers.
39. [ ] Generating and sending monthly success/progress reports.
40. [ ] Processing subscription changes or cancellations in your billing tool.

### [ ] SECTION 5: DATA ANALYSIS & REPORTING
41. [ ] Exporting CSVs from different sources just to merge them in Excel.
42. [ ] Building "Pivot Tables" manually to see monthly performance.
43. [ ] Cleaning up trailing spaces, case-styles, or formatting in data exports.
44. [ ] Calculating ROI, LTV, or CAC in a spreadsheet manually every month.
45. [ ] Building/Updating an executive dashboard manually every Monday morning.
46. [ ] Syncing contact information between your CRM and Billing software.
47. [ ] Identifying "At Risk" (Churn) clients by manually scanning usage data.
